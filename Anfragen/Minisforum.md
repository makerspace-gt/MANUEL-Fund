# Discount request from a nonprofit makerspace for a mini PC for a local AI project

eu@minisforum.com

Dear Minisforum team,

Makerspace Gütersloh e.V. is a nonprofit open workshop in Gütersloh, Germany, where members and guests explore and use machines and tools together. Our motto is »Do whatever you want!«, and we encourage everyone to be creative and build new things. Our members range from curious beginners to professionals, and we help each other with projects nobody could manage alone. We see ourselves as a sharing community that gives many people in our rural region access to machines, digital equipment and shared knowledge. Our workshop covers electronics, repair, 3D printing, textiles, photo, audio, video, virtual reality, IT, woodworking and laser cutting. The association is run entirely by about 45 volunteers.

Since March 2019 we have held a monthly Repair Café together with the Gütersloh city library. There we help people repair broken items or install Linux, and take a stand against throwaway culture.

We are now looking for hardware for our project MANUEL (Minimale AI für Nutzer-Unterstützung auf Einstiegs-Level, roughly »Minimal AI for entry-level user support«). MANUEL is a locally hosted large language model connected to Zammad, the ticket system we use for our Repair Café. It will categorize repair requests, make initial diagnoses and suggest fixes. This makes it easier for guests and new volunteers with little experience to take part. All request data has to stay with us, so we run the models locally instead of in the cloud.

For this we need a mini PC with an AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX or Ryzen AI Max+ and 64 GB of RAM that can later be upgraded to 128 GB. The MINISFORUM AI X1 Pro-370 with 64 GB RAM and a 2 TB SSD fits our needs best.

We are also interested in the MS-S1 MAX. Its memory is soldered, so only the 128 GB version would work for us.

As a volunteer-run nonprofit we have very limited funds. We would therefore like to ask whether you could offer us a discount on the device. A refurbished unit would also be perfectly fine for us. Giving devices a second life is what our Repair Café is all about.

In return we would gladly credit Minisforum as a supporter in our project documentation, on our website and on our social media channels. When the project is finished, we will publish MANUEL as an openly documented model project for other repair initiatives to reuse. That way others can also benefit from what we learn with your hardware.

We can send you our tax exemption certificate on request.

We look forward to hearing from you.
